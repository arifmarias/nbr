Two workstreams, one pilot

IWM sees the ground. TaxSight decides, acts and scales.

IWM · the eyes

Geospatial survey

LiDAR, drone photogrammetry, mobile mapping, satellite and Mouza maps. Every building in Zone 10 gets an ID and a Declared, Under-declared or Undeclared tag.

Output: a 3D building registry linked to owner and tax records.

TaxSight · the brain and the hands

AI and scalable big data platform

Find hidden owners, floors, rent and businesses across 10+ agency datasets

Value rent and tax owed per building, with a range

Prioritise by expected recovery per officer hour

Act with Bangla notices, case files and a field app

Output: ranked, explainable, ready-to-send cases. Built for 25 zones on day one.

Both teams work for NBR on the same 12 weeks. We do not compete with IWM. We make their pilot sell itself.

How it works

A loop that gets sharper every cycle.

1

Find

Entity resolution and anomaly detection across agency data.

2

Value

AI rent model. Taka gap per building with a confidence range.

3

Prioritise

"Revenue at Risk" ranks who gets a notice first.

4

Act

GenAI drafts the notice in Bangla. The officer reviews and signs.

5

Learn

Every visit and appeal retrains the models. Hit rates rise.

1 rule

AI recommends, a human officer decides. Every score comes with reasons a taxpayer and a court can understand.

Delta Lake on Spark

Sedona and PostGIS for city-scale spatial joins.

Design budgets from per-building compute costs (about 1.4 GPU-seconds and 46 CPU-seconds per building). To be confirmed on pilot data. National is a planning case of 120 zone-equivalents.

Governance and responsible AI
